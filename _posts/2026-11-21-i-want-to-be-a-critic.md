As a chronic people-pleaser, it's refreshing to hear a celebration of honest critique:

> I have to think of ways to un-become an it-girl. My first evil thought is that I should write a bad review to remind people I’m a critic. Like, come on, do not Photoshop me next to Katy Hessel. I’m not here to advertise. I’m not here to cheerlead every team so everyone likes me and, well, calls me an it-girl. I want to always be the one in the suit telling the team exactly why they just lost another game. I want the players to apologise to me one by one in the changing room. I want them to say they’ll do better next time. I want to doubt they will.

- [Gabrielle de la Puente, The White Pube](https://thewhitepube.co.uk/texts/2026/scene-city/)
